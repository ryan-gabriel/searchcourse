# Design Direction: SearchCourse

## Identity

SearchCourse is a course-deal platform for self-learners. The design is utilitarian with a holiday-season identity: red for deal urgency, deep green for value and learning, cool neutral base.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#f3f4f6` | Cool neutral base |
| Foreground | `#14181c` | Near-black, cool tinted |
| Card | `#ffffff` | Surface lift |
| Muted | `#e5e7eb` | Badge backgrounds, hover states |
| Muted foreground | `#4b5563` | Secondary text (7.5:1 on base) |
| Border | `#d1d5db` | Hairline dividers |
| Accent | `#cb0b0a` | Primary CTA, discount badges, deal signal |
| Destructive | `#8e0413` | Errors, rating stars, pressed states |
| Secondary | `#004733` | Evergreen header/footer, Free/verified badges |
| Support | `#568d66` | Decorative only: SectionRule motif, badge backgrounds |

Two color families (red = urgency, green = value) on a neutral base. One accent moment: the red CTA.

## Typography

Geist Sans (primary) and Geist Mono (code). Typefaces chosen for readability and utilitarian character.

## Dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Motif

The pine-needle SectionRule appears under section headings. A thin `--color-support` (#568d66) horizontal line with three upward strokes at intervals, suggesting a pine branch. Used exactly once per section heading on the homepage.

## Christmas identity

The evergreen header and footer bands (`--color-secondary: #004733`) with white text and light logos create a restrained holiday feel. No seasonal decoration (no snowflakes, no Santa). The Christmas comes from the color, not from motifs or ornaments.
